## 3. Agreement Guidelines

1. **Meetings:** attend the weekly team meeting; tell the team at least 3 hours before if absent.
2. **Deadlines:** internal deadlines are 2 days before official ones; report any delay to the leader immediately.
3. **Quality:** every document is reviewed by another member before submission.
4. **Communication:** reply to team messages within 24 hours (Telegram group).
5. **Version control:** all files are committed to the team GitHub repository with clear messages; nobody overwrites another member's work without agreement.
6. **Honesty:** all work is the team's own; sources and tools used are cited.
7. **Decisions:** by consensus, otherwise majority vote; the leader breaks ties.
8. **Conflicts:** discussed first between the people involved, then in the team, then with the instructor if unresolved.
9. **Changes:** changes to scope or deadlines need the approval of all three members and are recorded in the repository.
